Design principles

Prescription ≠ dispensing

What was ordered and what was supplied are different events.

Dispensing ≠ ingestion

Do not infer that a patient took a medicine from a pharmacy record.

AI supports clinicians

Summaries should show sources, dates, uncertainty, and missing data.

QR is not a public medical file

Real access requires provider authentication, least privilege, expiry, and revocation.
