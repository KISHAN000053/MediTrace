# Meditrace — Hackathon & Fundraising Pitch

## Idea title

**Meditrace — AI-Powered QR Healthcare Records and Medication Tracking**

## Detailed description (plain English)

Every time a patient visits a new doctor, they may have to explain their medical history again or search for old prescriptions, test reports, and medicine details. Health information is often scattered across paper files and different clinics. Doctors may not know which treatments were tried before, whether symptoms have returned, or what medicines a pharmacy actually supplied. A prescription alone does not show whether the medicine was dispensed or taken.

Meditrace addresses this problem by giving patients a digital medical record and a secure, QR-based way to share it with authorized healthcare providers. With the patient's permission, a doctor can review previous visits, diagnoses, allergies, prescriptions, medicines, and lab reports, then add new findings and treatment instructions to the patient's timeline.

When a patient visits a pharmacy, the pharmacist can use the same consent-based workflow to view the relevant prescription and record the medicines actually dispensed. Meditrace keeps the doctor's prescription separate from the pharmacy's dispensing record, so a future doctor can see what was ordered and what the available records show was supplied. Missing dispensing information is treated as a question to clarify—not proof that the patient did not take the medicine.

Before a future consultation, Meditrace's AI can analyze the available medical timeline and generate a concise summary of previous visits, recorded symptoms, medicines, lab results, and treatment changes. It can highlight recurring documented symptoms, gaps in the record, and differences between prescriptions and recorded dispensing events. The summary links back to the source records so the doctor can verify it and remains responsible for diagnosis and treatment.

By connecting patients, doctors, pharmacies, and laboratories through a patient-controlled record, Meditrace aims to reduce paperwork, save time spent reconstructing medical history, and improve continuity of care. Its design direction emphasizes privacy, time-limited consent, and offline access for people with unreliable internet. Secure data exchange between different devices, reliable synchronization, and clinical validation are key engineering requirements before real-world deployment.

## Short application description

Meditrace is an AI-powered QR healthcare platform connecting patients, doctors, pharmacies, and laboratories. It organizes medical records, distinguishes prescribed medicines from those recorded as dispensed, and generates source-linked AI summaries to support better-informed consultations and continuity of care.

## Strong one-line pitch

**Meditrace connects a patient's healthcare journey through secure QR-based sharing, transparent medication records, and AI summaries that help doctors understand the history faster.**

## Problem → solution → differentiation → impact

- **Problem:** Medical history is fragmented, and each visit can require patients and doctors to reconstruct previous care manually.
- **Solution:** A patient-controlled digital timeline shared through secure, time-limited QR consent.
- **Differentiation:** Prescription and pharmacy dispensing are stored as separate events; AI summarizes history with links to source records.
- **Impact sought:** Less paperwork, faster access to relevant history, better-informed follow-up questions, and stronger continuity of care.

## Demo story

1. A patient opens Meditrace and shares a time-limited consent QR.
2. A doctor reviews the timeline and records a visit and prescription.
3. A pharmacist records which medicines were actually dispensed, including a partial fill if applicable.
4. At the next visit, the doctor opens an AI summary that shows the prescription and dispensing records separately, highlights missing information, and links to source entries.
5. The doctor verifies the details with the patient and makes the clinical decision.

## Fundraising note

The opportunity is not simply digitizing prescriptions. Meditrace aims to create a longitudinal, patient-controlled record across care settings and make that record useful at the point of care. The key validation milestones are reliable consent and data exchange, privacy/security review, clinically grounded summaries, and measurable reductions in history-gathering time.

## Important claims discipline

Do not claim that Meditrace diagnoses disease, proves medication adherence, guarantees improved outcomes, or is fully compliant/production-ready without evidence. A dispensing record does not prove ingestion, and absence of a record does not prove a medicine was not obtained. Present AI as clinician-reviewed decision support.
